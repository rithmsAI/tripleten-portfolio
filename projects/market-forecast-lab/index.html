Provider snapshots can be revised. The bundled results are retrospective, not a point-in-time institutional data feed.

What does the model predict?

At completed bar t, nine price and volume features forecast close/open − 1 for the next observed session. Stocks follow observed trading sessions; crypto uses daily UTC buckets. This is a return forecast, not an exact price target or a probability of winning.

How should I interpret uncertainty?

Absolute errors from a separate past calibration block define a nominal 90% residual interval. Dependence and market changes can break coverage. The dashboard reports observed coverage rather than claiming a guarantee.

What are the research limits?

Four selected surviving assets and a small historical test window cannot establish a general market edge. News, liquidity, taxes, latency, market impact, portfolio allocation and broker execution are not modeled. Sandbox changes reuse observed outcomes and need a new untouched forward test before any performance claim.
